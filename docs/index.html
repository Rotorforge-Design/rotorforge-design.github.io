01 / What is RotorForge?

From your idea
to your frame.

RotorForge is a dedicated FreeCAD workbench for FPV pilots, makers and frame designers who want more control over their hardware. Instead of modeling every plate and arm from scratch, configure the geometry in a purpose-built editor and generate the assembly as a FreeCAD model.

Design the carbon structure, configure component mounting and create matching printable protection. Revisit your parameters whenever your ideas change.

RotorForge generates design geometry, not a certified aircraft or a flight-performance simulation. Always verify your final design before manufacturing and flying.

02 / Capabilities

Everything starts with the frame.

A purpose-built toolkit for the geometry, hardware, protection and manufacturing details of a custom FPV build.

Frame geometry

Choose True-X, Squashed-X or Deadcat layouts. Set the wheelbase, proportions and overall footprint.

Carbon plates & arms

Build two- or three-plate structures with configurable arms, fitted joints, motor pads, clamps and mounting hardware.

Cutouts & personalization

Create custom shapes, pockets, airflow patterns and lettering. Refine the outline and minimum material settings.

FPV camera systems

Configure camera mounts, tilt, isolation and analog or digital camera references. Inspect the virtual camera view.

Custom TPU protection

Design printable camera cages, front bumpers, motor protection and side guards around your frame.

Battery & LiPo pads

Set battery position, strap slots, pad geometry, grip patterns and lettering. Preview the battery within the assembly.

Rear mounting systems

Configure VTX decks, antenna mounts, ELRS supports

References & design checks

Inspect electronics, motors, props and hardware in context, with estimated mass, center of gravity and clearance checks.

Manufacturing export

Prepare DXF and SVG cutting profiles, arranged CNC layouts, STEP assemblies and STL/STEP printable parts.

04 / The workflow

Build it, step by step.

Keep your design decisions connected from the first layout through the final export.

01 — CONFIGURE

Define the frame

Choose a layout, plate structure, arm proportions and mounting arrangement.

02 — REFINE

Make it yours

Adjust cutouts, camera placement, TPU protection, battery pad and component references.

03 — INSPECT

Review the assembly

Inspect the generated geometry, estimated mass, clearances and camera view. Save the project and settings.

04 — EXPORT

Prepare to make

Export the appropriate files for CNC machining, 3D printing or further CAD work.

08 / Common questions

Good to know.

Is RotorForge free?

Yes. RotorForge is free and open source under the MIT license. FreeCAD is also free and open-source software.

Is RotorForge a standalone application?

No. It is an add-on workbench that runs inside FreeCAD.

Can I change a frame after it is created?

Yes. Open the saved FreeCAD document and use Edit frame to modify the RotorForge parameters and regenerate the assembly. You can also save and load parameter settings as JSON.

Can I use it for CNC machining?

RotorForge provides cutting profiles, 3D geometry and manufacturing-related output. You still need to verify machining settings, dimensions and toolpaths before cutting.

Does it simulate drone flight or guarantee strength?

No. The generated model and visualizations are design aids; they do not validate structural strength, vibration behavior or flight safety.

Where can I report a bug or request a feature?

Open an issue in the RotorForge GitHub repository. Include your version, FreeCAD version, screenshots and reproduction steps if possible.
